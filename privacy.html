Privacy Policy

SunCast · Effective and last updated September 27, 2026

The short version
  • SunCast has no account and no sign-in. The developer runs no server and receives none of your data.
  • Your location is used only to get the forecast for where you are. It is sent to the weather service Open-Meteo and to Apple, and nowhere else.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking across apps or websites, and your data is never sold.

Who we are

SunCast is made by Omar Saraireh, an independent developer based in Canada ("we", "us"). If you have any question about this policy or your data, email omorasaraira@gmail.com.

What SunCast uses and why

Your location

If you allow it, SunCast reads your location to forecast the sky where you are and to calculate your local sunrise, sunset and golden hour.

  • Only while you use the app. SunCast asks for "While Using the App" access. It takes a single reading when it needs one and does not track you continuously or read your location in the background.
  • Approximate is enough. SunCast only needs your position to within about a kilometre, so it works fine if you turn off Precise Location.
  • It's optional. You can say no and search for a city instead.

Where your location goes

  • Open-Meteo (weather data). To get your forecast, the app sends your coordinates (latitude and longitude, rounded to 4 decimal places) to Open-Meteo's forecast and air-quality services. No name, account, device ID or other identifier is sent with them. Like any website or service you connect to, Open-Meteo also sees your device's IP address.
  • Apple (place names and city search). SunCast uses Apple's MapKit to turn your coordinates into a place name such as "Lisbon", and to look up cities you type into search. Apple processes these requests under its own privacy policy.

If you pick a city instead, the same thing happens with that city's coordinates.

What stays on your device

  • The last place you viewed (its name, coordinates and time zone) and its forecast, so the app opens instantly and the widgets can show the same scores.
  • Your heads-up settings (whether alerts are on, your score threshold and whether you want sunrise alerts).

This information is stored only on your iPhone. We never receive it.

Notifications

Heads-up alerts are off by default. If you turn them on, SunCast asks for notification permission and schedules the alerts on your device. There is no push server, and we never receive your notification settings.

Background refresh

If Background App Refresh is on, SunCast refreshes the forecast for your last saved place about every two hours, so your widgets and alerts stay current. The widgets can also refresh the forecast themselves if the app hasn't in a while. This sends that place's coordinates to Open-Meteo, as described above. It does not read your location in the background.

Open-Meteo says it keeps server logs, including requested coordinates, for 90 days and does not share them with third parties.

What we don't do

  • No account, sign-in or personal profile.
  • No advertising and no ad networks.
  • No analytics, crash-reporting or tracking software.
  • No tracking across other companies' apps or websites.
  • We never sell, rent or trade your data.

If you choose to share diagnostics with app developers in your iPhone's settings, Apple may give us anonymous crash reports and usage statistics. You can change this in Settings > Privacy & Security > Analytics & Improvements.

How long data is kept, and deleting it

  • On your device: the saved place, forecast and settings stay until you change them or delete SunCast. Deleting the app (and its widgets) removes all of it.
  • With us: nothing. We have no server and store no data about you.
  • With Open-Meteo: as described above, their server logs are kept for up to 90 days.

Permissions and how to turn them off

  • Location: Settings > Privacy & Security > Location Services > SunCast. Choose "Never" or turn off Precise Location.
  • Notifications: Settings > Notifications > SunCast, or turn off "Give me a heads-up" in the app.
  • Background App Refresh: Settings > General > Background App Refresh > SunCast.

Children's privacy

SunCast is suitable for all ages but is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). Because the app has no account and we receive no data, there is no personal information about a child for us to hold. If you think a child has sent us personal information by email, contact us and we'll delete it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, laws such as Canada's PIPEDA, the EU and UK GDPR and California's CCPA give you rights over your personal information, including to access it, correct it, delete it, or object to how it is used. Because SunCast doesn't send us any of your data, we usually have nothing to access or delete, but we'll gladly answer any question. Email omorasaraira@gmail.com and we'll reply within 30 days.

For data held by Open-Meteo or Apple, please contact them using the links above. You may also complain to your local data protection authority, such as the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

This website

This website uses no cookies, analytics, trackers or third-party scripts. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub may log visitors' IP addresses for security purposes; see the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Changes to this policy

If SunCast's data practices change, we'll update this page and the date at the top before the change takes effect. For significant changes, we'll also mention it in the app's release notes on the App Store.
