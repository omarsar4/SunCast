Frequently asked questions

How is the score calculated?

SunCast looks at the forecast for the minutes around sunrise or sunset: high and mid-level cloud (which catch the colour), low cloud (which blocks the horizon), rain, recent clearing after rain, visibility, humidity and haze. Each one pushes the score up or down. Tap any forecast to see what moved it most.

The sky looked different from the score. Why?

The score is a forecast, not a guarantee. Clouds can form or clear quickly, and SunCast reads the forecast at your location, not out on the horizon toward the sun. Treat the score as a guide to when it's worth heading out.

Do I have to share my location?

No. If you'd rather not, tap the place name at the top of the app and search for a city. If you do share it, approximate location is enough. See the privacy policy for exactly where your location goes.

It says "Location is off." How do I turn it on?

Open Settings > Privacy & Security > Location Services, make sure Location Services is on, then tap SunCast and choose "While Using the App".

I'm not getting heads-up alerts.
  • Check that "Give me a heads-up" is on (tap the place name at the top of the app).
  • Check Settings > Notifications > SunCast and allow notifications.
  • Alerts are only sent when a sunrise or sunset scores at or above your threshold (60, 70 or 80%).
  • Sunrise alerts are optional and are sent at 9 PM the evening before.
  • To keep alerts current without opening the app, turn on Settings > General > Background App Refresh for SunCast.
My widget isn't up to date.

Widgets refresh about every hour, and iOS decides exactly when. Opening SunCast refreshes them straight away. Low Power Mode and turning off Background App Refresh can slow updates down.

Why does it say "No sunset today"?

Near the poles there are days when the sun never sets or never rises. SunCast shows this instead of a score.

Does SunCast need an internet connection?

Yes, to download new forecasts. It keeps your last forecast on your device, so you can still see recent scores while offline.

Which devices are supported?

iPhone with iOS 26 or later.

Where does the weather data come from?

From Open-Meteo.com, which combines forecasts from national weather services. Their data is licensed under CC BY 4.0.

How do I delete my data?

Everything SunCast saves stays on your iPhone. Deleting the app removes it all. There's no account, and the developer holds no data about you.
